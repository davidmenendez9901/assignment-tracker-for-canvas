Assignment Tracker for Canvas

Shows scores, points left, and deadlines on Canvas course Assignments pages. It only reads data from the Canvas site you are on, using your existing login. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Use at your own risk

This is a free, independent tool provided “as is”, without any warranty. It shows what Canvas reports, but it can be wrong or out of date. Canvas is the official source: always check your deadlines and grades there. The developer is not responsible for missed deadlines, grades, or any other consequence of using it.
