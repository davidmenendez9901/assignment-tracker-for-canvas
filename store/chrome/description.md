## Description

Assignment Tracker for Canvas helps students see where they stand in every course, right on the Canvas Assignments page.

WHAT YOU GET
• A badge on every assignment: "85/100 · 15 pts left", Overdue, Missing, Late (−5), Closed, Opens Mar 20, Submitted · awaiting grade, Grade not posted, Excused, Retry available.
• A collapsible summary at the top of the page with counts and filters: Not at 100%, Open, Overdue, Closed, Pending grading.
• Ungraded and 0-point assignments are never counted as missing points.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Reads only from the Canvas site you're on, with your existing login. No passwords or API tokens.
• Read-only: it cannot submit, edit, or delete anything.
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no remote code. Nothing leaves your browser.
• Open source (MIT): https://github.com/davidmenendez9901/assignment-tracker-for-canvas

ACCESSIBLE
• Text and icons, not color alone. Screen-reader labels, keyboard navigation, dark page and high-contrast support.

WORKS WITH
• Canvas sites on *.instructure.com automatically.
• Your school's custom Canvas address: click the toolbar icon on that site and choose "Enable on this site".
